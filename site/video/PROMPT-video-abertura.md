# Vídeo de abertura do site G2

Salve o arquivo final como `abertura.mp4` nesta pasta. O site detecta o arquivo e mostra o vídeo automaticamente no topo, antes do título. Sem o arquivo, a seção fica oculta.

Especificação: 16:9, 1920x1080, 10 a 15 segundos, sem áudio (o site toca mudo), loop limpo (último frame volta ao primeiro). Até 8 MB. Se o gerador entregar em 5 segundos, gere 2 ou 3 cenas e junte.

## Prompt principal (inglês, para Veo, Runway, Kling, Higgsfield, Sora)

> Cinematic macro footage inside a modern sock knitting factory. A circular knitting machine spins, hundreds of fine off-white polyamide yarns feed from cones into steel needles. Extreme close-up of needles pulling thread into a tight mesh, a running sock forming in real time, a thin dark red reinforcement band appearing at the heel. Slow dolly along the yarn lines, shallow depth of field, soft industrial daylight from high windows, dust particles in the light. Color palette: off-white, charcoal black, one accent of deep red. Clean, quiet, premium. No people, no text, no logos. 24fps, slow motion, seamless loop.

## Variações, se quiser escolher

**Mais abstrato, só o fio**
> Extreme macro of a single off-white polyamide thread traveling through steel guides and tensioners, catching light like a wire. The thread meets a knitting needle and becomes mesh. Black background, one red thread crosses the frame at the end. Slow, precise, seamless loop.

**Mais produto, meia pronta**
> Macro studio shot, a finished running sock in off-white with red heel and toe, slowly rotating on a dark charcoal surface. Camera glides over the ribbed cuff, the ventilated mesh on the top, the arch compression band. Soft key light from the left, subtle rim light. No text, no hands. Seamless loop.

**Mais corrida, uso real**
> Low angle slow motion of a runner's feet on wet asphalt at dawn, wearing off-white running socks with a red heel, ankle-height. Focus stays on the sock as the foot lands and lifts, water droplets flying. Muted tones, overcast light, one red accent. No face, no logos. Seamless loop.

## Palavras para o campo "negative" ou "evitar"
text, logo, watermark, people faces, hands, bright colors, neon, cotton socks, cluttered background, fast cuts, music video style.

## Dica
Peça sempre "seamless loop" e "no text". Para juntar cenas e cortar, iMovie ou CapCut resolvem. Exporte em H.264, 1080p, sem áudio.

## Hero "tecido": macro da malha da meia (prompt recomendado)

Objetivo: o visitante entender em 3 segundos que isso não é meia de algodão. Só textura, luz e um toque de vermelho.

**Prompt principal**
> Ultra macro cinematic shot of a high-performance running sock fabric, off-white high-tenacity polyamide knit. The camera starts almost touching the surface, so close that individual loops of the knit read like architecture, then pulls back and glides slowly across the sock revealing three zones: a dense ribbed compression band at the arch, an open ventilated mesh on the top of the foot, and a thick cushioned terry at the heel with a deep red reinforcement thread woven through it. Light rakes across the surface at a low angle from the left, so every loop casts a tiny shadow; a subtle rim light outlines the edge of the fabric. The fabric flexes slightly, as if a foot is breathing inside it, and a few drops of water bead on the mesh and vanish, absorbed and pushed out. Background is pure charcoal black, nothing else in frame. Palette: off-white, charcoal, one accent of deep red. Probe lens, extreme shallow depth of field, 100mm macro, slow dolly, 24fps, 120fps slow motion feel. Premium, quiet, tactile, like a sportswear brand film. No people, no hands, no text, no logos. Seamless loop, last frame matches first.

**Variação curta (5 s, para gerar em partes)**
> Cena 1: Extreme macro, the camera pushes into off-white knitted polyamide until single loops fill the frame, raking side light, charcoal background, slow motion.
> Cena 2: Slow lateral glide over the sock surface, ribbed band transitions into open mesh, a red thread crosses the frame at the heel, water beads on the mesh and disappears.
> Cena 3: Pull back as the fabric flexes once, like a foot landing, and settles, ending on the same framing as cena 1.

**Negative / evitar**
cotton texture, fuzzy fibers, lint, bright colors, neon, hands, feet with skin, people, text, logo, watermark, fast cuts, blurry, low detail, plastic look, shiny latex.
